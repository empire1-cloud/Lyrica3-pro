# 90-Day Proof Plan

## Objective

Prove one creator-owned loop works:

Lyrica 3 creator flow -> generation -> DNA / IP proof -> Archisynapse payment and fraud layer -> tester feedback -> investor evidence

## Days 1-30

Focus: clean demo and first tester proof

Targets:

- 7 to 10 testers
- one stable creator flow
- one repeatable demo script
- clear screenshot/video evidence
- one usable investor narrative

Execution:

- lock the product to one narrow use case
- remove or disable unfinished public-facing demo paths
- keep billing off unless fully production-ready
- run tester sessions with the same script
- capture confusion points and fix the top 3

Success criteria:

- testers can complete the basic flow
- the DNA / proof layer is understandable
- the product loads reliably
- investor materials can show real evidence, not concept slides

## Days 31-60

Focus: widen from proof to repeatability

Targets:

- 25 to 50 users or qualified waitlist leads
- better onboarding
- clearer payment/risk story
- improved evidence of retention or repeat usage

Execution:

- tighten onboarding copy and first-use flow
- improve reliability and logging
- identify the most credible creator segment
- turn tester feedback into short product iterations
- define the minimum viable billing/payout path

Success criteria:

- multiple users complete the loop without live hand-holding
- payment/fraud logic is legible to non-technical users
- at least some users return or request follow-up access
- the product story is becoming easier, not harder, to explain

## Days 61-90

Focus: convert proof into commercial signal

Targets:

- 50 paying creators or pilot accounts
- one retention signal worth reporting
- one investor update with real numbers

Execution:

- convert the strongest early segment first
- formalize pilot or paid access terms
- publish a concise operating dashboard:
  - testers
  - active users
  - completed creator loops
  - payment/risk events
  - pilot or paid conversions
- prepare next-round materials from actual usage

Success criteria:

- there is a credible path from proof to revenue
- product messaging is consistent
- investors can see a measurable system, not only a vision

## What not to do during these 90 days

- do not expand into multiple new universes
- do not keep renaming the system
- do not add major architecture for optics
- do not ship unfinished demo layers publicly
- do not broaden the story before the first loop is trusted

## Core operating principle

Keep the system bigger internally than it looks externally.

Externally, the market should see one sharp thing:

"A creator-owned AI music workflow with proof, payment intelligence, and a path to monetization."
